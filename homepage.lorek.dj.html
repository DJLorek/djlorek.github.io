Disk-Jockey

Between 2018 and 2020, I attended a professional DJ course at Goody Music DJ School in Rome, under the guidance of DJ Aimè.

That experience gave me a solid technical and artistic foundation, covering mixing techniques and equipment handling.

After the pandemic I began my real field experience, performing at high school events across Rome.

One of my main strengths as a DJ is my ability to move across different musical genres, even outside of the EDM subgroup.
