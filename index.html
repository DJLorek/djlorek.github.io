About

Hi, I'm DJ Lorek, an Italian DJ and electronic dance music producer set in the city of Rome since 2018.

I started making music as a hobby and then transitioned to using it to represent the world.

I love experimenting with different synths and genres, whether it's Melodic EDM, Techno or House Music my goal has always been the same:

Creating something meaningful and make people dance along with it.
